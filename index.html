ProKids Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026

In short:

  • ProKids is a parental control app. A parent installs it on their own device and on their child's device, and sees the child's device activity in the parent dashboard.
  • We collect only what the app needs to work. We do not show ads, we do not sell data, and we do not use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Information from a child's device is visible only to the parent account it is paired with.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and all its data at any time — see Deleting your account and data.

2. How ProKids works

ProKids has two roles inside one app:

  • Parent. An adult creates an account, pairs one or more child devices, and uses the dashboard to see activity and set rules.
  • Child device. The parent installs ProKids on the child's device, pairs it using a code or QR code, and grants the permissions the app asks for. The child does not create an account or enter personal details.

ProKids is intended for parents and legal guardians monitoring the devices of their own minor children. By pairing a device you confirm that you are the child's parent or legal guardian and that you consent to the collection described here on the child's behalf. ProKids must not be used to monitor another adult or anyone without legal authority to do so.

Monitoring is not hidden. The child's device shows ProKids in its app list, displays ongoing notifications while monitoring is running, and shows an indicator when the screen is being viewed.

4. Information we do not collect

  • No audio or microphone recording.
  • No photos, videos or files from the device. The camera is used only to scan the pairing QR code; no image is saved or uploaded.
  • No contacts, calendar, call logs, SMS or messages.
  • No web browsing history. Web filtering happens on the device and the list of visited sites is not sent to us.
  • No payment card details. Payments are handled by Google Play.
  • No advertising ID, no advertising, and no third-party analytics or tracking.

5. Device permissions and how they are used

The child's device is asked for several sensitive permissions. Each is used only for the purpose stated.

  • Location (including background location). Used to report the device's location to the paired parent account, even when the app is closed or not in use.
  • Accessibility Service. Used only to detect which app is currently in the foreground so that apps the parent has blocked, or all apps while the device is locked, are closed and the device returns to the home screen. ProKids does not read screen content, text or keystrokes through this service, and nothing obtained through it is sent off the device.
  • VPN service. ProKids creates a local, on-device VPN that handles only domain name (DNS) lookups, so that advertising domains and sites the parent has blocked do not load. Your browsing traffic is not routed through our servers and we do not receive a record of the sites visited. Lookups for sites that are not blocked are passed to a public DNS resolver (Cloudflare or Google), as any normal internet connection would do.
  • Usage access. Used to read which apps are used and for how long.
  • Screen capture. Used for Live View. Android asks for consent on the device, and capture is only transmitted while the parent is watching.
  • Camera. Used only to scan the pairing QR code.
  • Device administrator and notifications. Used to help keep monitoring active and to show that it is running.

6. How we use information

We use the information above for two purposes only:

  • Providing the app's features — showing activity, location and Live View to the parent, applying the rules the parent sets, and sending alerts.
  • Managing your account — sign-in, subscription status and support requests.

We do not use your information for advertising, marketing, profiling or analytics.

7. Who can see the information

Information from a child's device is visible to the parent account that device is paired with. Access is restricted on our servers so that one family cannot read another family's data.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with third parties for their own use. We use the following service providers, which process data only to run the app on our behalf:

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and real-time delivery.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Expo — delivery of push notifications.
  • Google Play — subscription purchases.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

8. Storage and security

Data is stored with our hosting provider on servers located in the European Union. All data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we limit access to what the app needs to function.

9. How long we keep information

We keep your account information and your children's device information for as long as your account exists. Removing a child device from the parent dashboard deletes that device's location and activity records. Live View frames are never stored.

10. Deleting your account and data

You can ask us to delete your ProKids account and all data linked to it.

  • Send an email to khalifa660@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my ProKids account".
  • We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Deleting your account removes your profile, your family, all paired devices, and all location history, activity logs, screen time records, alerts, rules and subscription records linked to them. Nothing is kept afterwards, except where we are legally required to retain it.

To delete the data of a single child device without closing your account, remove that device from the parent dashboard. Cancelling a subscription is done through Google Play and does not by itself delete your account.

11. Children's privacy

ProKids is designed for and marketed to parents. Children do not create accounts and are not asked for their name, email or other personal details. Information from a child's device is collected only after a parent or legal guardian installs the app on that device, pairs it and grants the permissions, and it is shown only to that parent.

If you believe a device is being monitored with ProKids without the consent of a parent or legal guardian, contact us and we will investigate and delete the data.

12. Your choices and rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about your account, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing us at the address above. You can stop collection from a child's device at any time by removing the device from the parent dashboard and uninstalling ProKids from that device.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how ProKids handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above.
